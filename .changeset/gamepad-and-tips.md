- **Gamepad support:** play with a standard controller. The left stick moves, the right stick aims, RT fires, LT sprints, the bumpers and Y use your abilities, the d-pad throws utilities and switches weapons, A interacts and revives, X reloads, B swaps and Start opens the menu.
- **Tips while you play:** the first time something matters (an empty magazine, a teammate down, an attack marked on the ground), a short tip tells you what to press. Each tip shows once. Turn them off, or back on, in the pause menu.
