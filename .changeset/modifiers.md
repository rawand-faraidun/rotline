- **Mission modifiers:** the leader can switch on harder rules beside the difficulty, for up to 60 % more rewards. The rules are Swarm (more zombies), Hardened (tougher zombies), Fragile (less health), Scarce ammunition, Blackout (the lights go out) and One life. The lobby shows what is on and the bonus.
- **Fixed:** the health bar measured against 100 instead of your real maximum, so it looked full until you were badly hurt.
