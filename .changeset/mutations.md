- **The horde mutates:** in Harrow, on Nightmare and Apocalypse, and every 8 rounds of Survival, zombies get tougher and gain traits. Each kind has its own order of traits: armoured, swift, toxic, regenerating, volatile, fireproof and insulated. The bestiary lists them, and the horde darkens with each stage.
- **Adaptive strains:** lean on one damage type and the horde answers. A strain hardened against it appears, drawn with a pale crust and announced on screen. Switch weapons.
- **Hardened** now pushes mutations one stage further.
