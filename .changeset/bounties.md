- **Weekly bounties:** each week one named elite haunts one campaign mission at one difficulty: Old Ironjaw, Mother Rot or The Whisper. Bring it down for 5,000 Salvage, once a week for everyone in the squad who helped, plus its title. The Records screen shows this week's bounty, and the mission picker marks its mission.
