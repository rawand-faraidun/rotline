- **The Gardener, Harrow's boss:** patient zero has grown into the botanical gardens. A new mission in the Glasshouse, open once Terminus is done. He lobs spore pods, brings the ground down round him and, when you keep your distance, sinks into the earth to burst out under you. He burns half again as fast, so bring fire. A new achievement and title come with him.
